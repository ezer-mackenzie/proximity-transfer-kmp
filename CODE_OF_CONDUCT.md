# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Focus technical disagreement on evidence and project goals.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it clearly.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and responsible security disclosure.
- Help create an environment where questions and differing levels of experience are welcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately unsafe technical conduct or public disclosure of unresolved vulnerabilities.
- Any behavior reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject participation, issue warnings, or impose temporary or permanent bans when behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
